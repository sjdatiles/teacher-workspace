# Manunudlo — User guide

## 1. First-time setup

1. **Sign up** with your email and a password, then open the verification email and click the link.
2. Go to **Settings → Academic years** and add your school year (for example 2026–2027). Enter the start and end dates of **Term 1, Term 2 and Term 3**. Terms cannot overlap and must be inside the year. Dates are yours to set; nothing is preset.
3. The bar at the top of every page shows the selected year and the **active term**. Use it to switch years.

## 2. My Classes

- **Subjects** and **Sections** (name, grade level, number of students). Only the _number_ of students is kept — no student records.
- Assign subjects to sections, then add a **Schedule** (day, start and end time). The app warns about overlaps and invalid times.
- A schedule creates a **session** for every matching date in its term(s). Add **No-class days** (holidays) so sessions skip them.
- **Sessions** tab: mark each one **Completed**, **Cancelled** or **Rescheduled**. You can edit the actual duration when completing. Use _Add session_ for make-up or extra classes. Past sessions start as Scheduled; bulk-complete them in one go.
- _Copy from last year_ brings your subjects and sections into a new year.

> **Only Completed sessions count** toward your totals, hours and reports.

## 3. Calendar & Planner

Month, Week and Day views show your sessions, events and no-class days. Add events (meeting, school activity, deadline, personal, other), choose a color, set a reminder or make them repeat. Editing a repeating event lets you change one date or the whole series.

## 4. Tasks

Tasks have a category, status (To Do / In Progress / For Review / Completed), priority (Low–Urgent) and optional due date. Switch between **List** and **Board**; filter and search. Tasks never affect your teaching reports.

## 5. Resources

Save web links (Google Drive, websites, references) into folders, mark favorites, add subject and tags, search and filter. Files cannot be uploaded; links only.

## 6. Reports

Pick **Term 1, 2, 3, Whole year** or a **custom date range**; optionally filter by subject or section. You see total sessions, total teaching hours and students taught, charts and a per-section table. **Download CSV** or **Print** (choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog).

## 7. Home and notifications

Home shows today's classes, upcoming items, task status and term progress. The bell lists upcoming sessions and events, deadlines and overdue tasks; mark items read or dismiss them. The **Quick actions** buttons open the matching add form.

## 8. Forgot your password

On the log-in page choose **Forgot password?**, enter your email and open the link we send **in the same browser** you requested it from. Choose a new password; you'll be signed out everywhere and asked to log in again. The message is the same whether or not the email has an account. Reset links expire; just request a new one.

## 9. Deleting your account

Settings → **Delete account** → _Delete my account…_. Enter your password and type `DELETE`. Your account and every record in it are erased immediately and cannot be recovered, so download any reports you need as CSV first.

## 10. Tips

- Set up the year and terms first; everything else depends on them.
- If a date shows "not in any term", fix your term dates in Settings.
- Your data is private to your account.
